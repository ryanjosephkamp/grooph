**What to take from LangGraph, and what not to**

- **Take:** replay and stepping through a run (its Studio does this for its own runtime); pausing for a person as a first-class thing (grooph's gates already are); small graphs inside graphs (grooph's fragments already are).
- **Leave:** a runtime. grooph runs nothing, and that is why it is light and works with harnesses people already have.
- **The difference in one line:** LangGraph is a library you write an agent program in. grooph is a drawing you hand to an agent you already have, with a checker in front of it.

I have not checked LangGraph's current features; that is on the Dot's list.
