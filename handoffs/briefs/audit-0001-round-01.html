The finding that matters most

The sentence on the README, the front page, the field guide and the report says grooph "is shown to bound and record autonomous work and to hold a design as a runtime contract". It comes from decision 0013. Both harnesses, reading separately, found that the first word is not carried.

not shownNo round cap and no budget has fired in any recorded run. In all 33 package runs at 0.3.0 the only stops that fired were a passed bar (18 times) and a human check-in (once). Study two, which ran since, added 24 runs and its own handback says the same: no brake fired.

misreadThe one run that ran on was not past a bound. In the first comparison a prompt-only run was cut off at the $9.00 ceiling. It was in its second round, on its fourth dispatch, inside the same caps the graph has (5 rounds, 12 dispatches). The ceiling was the runner's, and every arm had it.

shownRuns stopped where the graph said. Fourteen recorded halts at a human gate, and the kept runs ended at a passed bar, a stop the graph names, or a gate. The prompt arms halted at their gates too, when the prose told them to.

So what is shown is narrower than "bounds", and it is still worth saying. Whether a cap holds a run that would otherwise go on is simply not on record yet. One small experiment would put it there (answer 4 below).

The words I would publish

There are 26 numbered corrections in the reconciliation. This is the first, the status line of the README; the front page's version follows it closely.

Now

Twenty templates have each been proven in a recorded run, and one paired comparison has been made. On that evidence, grooph is shown to bound and record autonomous work and to hold a design as a runtime contract. It is not shown to raise quality over the same instructions given as a prompt, on small tasks.

Proposed

Each of the twenty templates has a recorded run: eighteen pass the project's checks and two are published red. In those runs a session stopped where its graph said, at a passed bar or at a human gate, and left a record of what it did. No round cap or budget is on record as firing, so it is not shown that one holds a run that would otherwise go on. In a paired comparison on four small tasks the package showed no quality advantage over a prompt derived from it.

The proposed line says nothing of study two on purpose. Its claims have been read inside Claude Code only. They go through round two before any page states them.
