Two grooph graphs in a plain page

Handoff 0056, criterion 8. Each frame below is the two lines grooph embed printed, pasted as they are. The page's own background shows through; the frames follow your light or dark setting.

A loop graph is a drawing of how agents work together, with the brakes on it. Here is one: a fast builder grinds against the tests inside each phase, and a stronger judge looks only at the end of a phase. Drag it, pinch or ctrl-scroll to zoom, and tap a node to read its brief.

And here is a run of the heterogeneous-critic template, recorded by Claude Code on 20 September. Press play: the critic fails the first round, the builder tries again, the bar passes in round 1, and the run stops at the human gate before anything is merged.
