# 0031 · Which models this project's own work uses, and why that is nobody else's rule

**Date:** 2026-10-05 · **Status:** accepted (the owner, in the driver's chat, 2026-10-05, in the words quoted below)

## Context

From 2026-10-04 the project's rules said "Never Fable. Never Astra." (`AGENTS.md`, decision 0024, the driver's notes). The owner has said what he meant. In his words: "I don't want us to run experiments where we use a Fable or Astra in any of the loop graphs or anything else. I don't want us to do reconciliation with Fable or Astra. The reason is very simple. Those models are expensive and they're not very token efficient." And: "We would never automatically use Fable or Astra for anything. But the key word there is automatically. If I want to override that, then I can tell you, and that's fine." And of everyone else: "That rule does not apply to other people who want to use our package, our product, our site, our loop graphs, anything like that. There are no restrictions about the models they use," and "it's extremely important that we do not place any artificial restrictions on the models that are allowed to be used for anything on a user."

## Decision

1. **This project's own work.** Its lanes, experiments, studies, audits and reconciliation do not use Fable or Astra unless the owner authorizes it ("never automatically"). He may authorize it at any time and for any use; he named two he can imagine, "maybe they'll get cheaper", and a study asking whether what was seen on the cheaper, faster models also holds on these. The driver records an authorization where it is used. The reason he gave is cost and token use; he made no claim about quality.
2. **Everyone else: no restriction.** grooph keeps no list of models. A person may name any model for any step of any graph, operation map or plan, by a tier map (`--models`, `GROOPH_MODELS`, `GROOPH_MODELS_CODEX`) or by a pin on a node, in the app or in the document, Fable and Astra among them; and a model helping them design a graph may suggest any model, those two included. The stock tier map (`frontier` Opus, `strong` and `fast` Sonnet for Claude Code) is a default and not a limit. The one thing grooph asks of a name is its shape: it must be one token (letters, digits and `. _ : / [ ] -`), so `gpt-6-astra` passes and `GPT-6 Astra`, with a space, does not. Whether a harness has the model is the harness's to say when it runs.
3. **Checked on 2026-10-05, by running the built command:** a tier map naming Fable for every tier, by flag and by environment variable; a node pinned to Fable in its document, which validates for export clean; Codex with GPT-6 Astra for every tier, by flag and by environment variable; and a name that exists nowhere. Each exported, exit 0, with the name written as given. Nothing in `packages/`, `apps/`, `plugins/`, the templates, the fixtures or the design skill refuses or steers away from either model; the sample bot-fleet map names GPT-6 Astra as a node's model. The only places in the repository that refuse them are this project's own experiment runners (`scripts/lib/compare-profile.mjs`, `compare-run.mjs`, `compare-ledger.mjs`, `prove-pattern.mjs`). They carry out point 1, their messages and comments now say whose policy it is, and they have no override: an authorized run is one where the authorization is recorded and the runner's pattern is changed in the same pull request.
4. **The owner's OpenAI dot (GPT-6 Astra).** In his words it takes no part in any experiment ("it's not going to be conducting any experiment or any trial … as a node or an operator or an agent"), and it is free this month so long as it hands no work to Codex or ChatGPT. A job of this project's own that needs no such hand-off may therefore go to it, if he says so. He has not, and he has said it may not be the right assistant for this work; nothing is briefed to it.

## Consequences

`AGENTS.md`, `handoffs/DRIVER.md`, `handoffs/README.md`, `docs/PLAN.md`, `docs/PROGRESS.md` and the fleets page say this in place of "never". Decision 0024's item 3 carries a dated note and is otherwise as signed. The Claude Code and Codex target pages say the tier maps are defaults, and `docs/graph-ir.md` says a tier is a class and not a named model. The design skill will say that a person's model choice is theirs and that suggesting any model is allowed, once the agents lane merges `main`; until then it says nothing about models and steers nowhere.
